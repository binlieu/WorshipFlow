# UI-005h: output looks (OUT, KEY, STAGE)

Satisfies: the design picture `docs/UI/screens/07-output-looks.png` and its mockup `07-output-looks.html`; extends OUT-003 (themes), STAGE-001 (stage layouts), KEY-001 (fill and key). Implemented and Unit Tested; Simulated only for the SDI readback. **No keyer, ATEM, converter or SDI signal has seen the lower third's matte: Not Verified on hardware.**

## Behaviour

1. **Type.** `TextStyle` gains `weight` (CSS 100 to 900), `letterSpacing` (em), `lineHeight` (a multiple of the size), `opticalSize` and `fontFeatures`; all optional, nil is the earlier look. A variable font (Inter) is set on its weight and optical-size axes; another font takes the nearest face of its family. A style with a line height is placed by its line boxes, like a web page (the block's edges sit on the frame; the text wraps at the frame's width; the padding for outline, shadow and marks hangs outside); a style without one is placed by its padded bitmap exactly as before.
2. **Audience.** The built-in Full screen theme is Inter 119.04 pt / 600 and 78.72 pt / 500 (second language, `#ffd17b`), a soft shadow (0, 5.76, blur 23.04, 60 %) and no outline, centred as one group, inside the 90 % title-safe width, over the design's radial gradient (`Theme.mediaFallback`). The shared loop still plays over the gradient; a slide's own background or a bin choice covers it; Clear background and a Look without the background layer drop it. `ThemeBackground.gradient` is a background kind of its own (the editor offers it with the design's colours).
3. **Lower third.** The built-in Lower third theme has a `TextPlate` (88 % wide, 96 pt above the bottom, radius 19.2, padding 46.08 x 30.72, fill `rgba(7,19,33,.86)`) and an `AccentBar` (11.52 wide, gradient `#42d9b8` to `#4d99ed`, 38.4 gap). The plated lines are stacked inside the plate, which grows upwards with them and keeps its bottom margin; no words, no plate. The **key matte is the alpha of the composed frame**: the plate's shape and opacity (86 % is grey 219, `#dbdbdb`), the opaque bar (white) and the opaque text over it, with premultiplied and straight alpha both split from the same frame (`FillKeySplitter`). The plate's rectangle is snapped to whole pixels (crisp edges, antialiased corners).
4. **Stage.** The built-in layout is the design's composition: current lyric (116.58 pt / 700), the second language in its own line (63.59 pt / 600, its lines joined with " · "), "NEXT" (26.5 pt, tracking .08 em) and the next line (67.12 pt / 700, `#9fb2c7`, no longer green), the clock (70.66 pt) and the first countdown timer (105.98 pt, `#49d3c3`, warning colour when over), and the stage message as a full-width warning band with dark text (`StageMessageBand`). The section label "CHORUS x2" is **not built**: the show state carries no section name or repeat count. Layouts the operator made are stored copies and are not changed; a layout without a message band keeps the large warning-coloured banner.
5. **Existing libraries.** Migration v13 (see the data dictionary) adds two themes, "Classic full screen" and "Classic lower third", holding the earlier built-in looks, to a library that already has content. Presentations that use a built-in theme (or none) change appearance on upgrade; the Classic themes bring the old look back per presentation. A church's own themes draw exactly the pixels they drew before.
6. **Everywhere.** Thumbnails, the Preview and Live monitors, the keyed previews in Screens and the real outputs all draw through `Compositor`, so each shows the new look. The SDI output now copies each frame to memory with a GPU copy into a shared buffer instead of `getBytes` (about 4 times faster for a picture that is not flat).

## FIX-J: changes after the independent review (2026-11-21)

Brief `AI/specs/FIX-J-ui-005h-review.md`, report `AI/reports/FIX-J-report.md`.

7. **Fit text to its block.** `Theme.fitsText` (on for the built-in looks; off for the Classic themes and for every stored theme, which therefore draw exactly as before; the theme editor has a "Shrink the words to fit their boxes" switch, default off). When on, a slide's layers carry `TextLayer.fitFloor` (56 pt at 1080 lines) and the compositor shrinks both languages by one factor, in 4 % steps, until each block's laid-out height and width are inside its frame (a plated lower-third block may take two lines at its design size first: reflow before shrinking). The floor is 56 pt for the first language. Words that still do not fit are drawn at the floor, clipped to the title-safe frame, and the slide is flagged: a warning mark on its thumbnail and the line "These words do not fit this theme at its smallest size" in the editor. The measure is made at the 1080-line reference, once per slide and theme (`Compositor.fitStatus`, `TextFit.swift`), so every output size and every thumbnail agrees on the factor. The audience theme's second-language frame reaches down to the title-safe bottom, as the first language's reaches up to the top.
8. **Text cache.** Least recently used entry first, one at a time; per-second text (`TextLayer.ticks`: the clock, timers, countdowns) has its own 16-entry cache; `TextWarmer` rasterises the selected slide for each screen on a background queue ahead of Go Live into a shared store the outputs' compositors read.
9. **Stored values.** `ColorRGBA`, `NormalizedRect`, `TextShadow`, `TextStyle`, `GradientBackground` (and its stops), `TextPlate` and `AccentBar` decode through their initialisers (clamps: line height 0.5 to 4, weight 100 to 900, optical size 6 to 144, letter spacing -0.5 to 2 em, radii, padding, margins, opacity, at least two sorted gradient stops, no zero radius).
10. **Tolerant loading.** A theme, Look or stage layout row that cannot be decoded is skipped (left in the table), the others load, and the operator is told once, by name where readable (`SkippedRow`).
11. **Stage parts.** The second-language line and the NEXT label are stored relative to their element (`relativeFrame`); layouts saved before this (`frame`, absolute) are converted when read, in memory.
12. **Inter check.** `OutputFont.check()` at launch; one notice if the output font is not the bundled Inter; the hardware-test environment records `outputFont`.
13. **Scripture limits** use the style's own line height and the measured average advance of its font and weight when the style has a line height of its own (the design's looks); the Classic and earlier themes keep the earlier estimate. A passage is split at the theme's design size, not at the floor.
14. **SDI.** `SDIOutput.isOpaque` knows a gradient background; a plate takes the opacity of the layers on it. The fill and key split was read and tested in both alpha modes (see the report): no defect found.
