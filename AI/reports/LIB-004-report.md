## Decisions where the brief left room
1. **Versification is a first-class object** (`Versification.kjv`, `.vi1925`, widenable), chosen per translation; the parser validates against the table you give it. Coordinator decision.
2. **Vietnamese context for abbreviations.** `BookMatcher` has `.auto`, `.english`, `.vietnamese`. Under `.auto`, an owner abbreviation that is also the start of other English names is ambiguous (`exo`, `dan`, `es`, `ma`, `phi`, `phil`, `ha`, `ca`, `so`); an English exact abbreviation (`Ex`, `Gen`) beats Vietnamese prefixes; text with Vietnamese diacritics is read as Vietnamese. The app should pass `.vietnamese` or `.english` from its UI language to avoid constant ambiguity prompts.
3. Vietnamese **display names** are the owner's 1925 names with Arabic numerals ("Sáng-thế Ký", "Thi-thiên", "1 Cô-rinh-tô"); any hyphenation, diacritics and Roman numerals are accepted when typing.
4. A book with no chapter ("John") is an error, not the whole book. `John 3,5` means chapters 3 and 5. A single-chapter book takes verses ("Jude 1" is verse 1).
5. Ranges that overlap or touch merge; order given is kept. A bad reference does not stop the others.
6. The reference is a separate slide field (`SlideContent` has no reference field); verse numbers are plain "16 " or superscript "¹⁶ ".
7. Pairing is by reference number only; the verses shown come from the primary translation's table (so 3 John 15 in a 1925 secondary is not shown against a KJV primary). A split verse is split into the same number of parts on both sides; the shorter side gets empty (nil) parts.
8. A translation file keeps verses numbered past its table and widens its own table, reporting the count; unknown versification names fall back to KJV with a message.
9. USFM `\d` (Psalm titles) is not kept; a bridge `\v 1-2` is stored at verse 1 and reported; unknown markers are counted and their following words are kept.
10. `ownerApproval {approved, date, note}` is optional metadata (coordinator request).
