# FIX-I: the owner's hands-on findings on build 101: keyboard, backgrounds, the warning card

Feature IDs: UI-005j, MEDIA-010, CUE (keys). Model: Sonnet, then an independent review because it changes what keys do in a live service. One agent owns all files; it starts after UI-005h is committed. No migration expected.

Source: the owner tested build 101 (commit 38ca00d) by hand on 2026-11-21. Their words are quoted; the decisions under each come from two questions put to them the same day.

## Items

1. **"Previous / Next with keyboard buttons doesn't work".** A regression: the arrow keys and probably Space did nothing in Show. Find the cause before changing anything and state it in the report. Suspects: FIX-H item 7 (a focused control takes Space, Return and the arrows) together with UI-005j's app-drawn toolbar and the design probe's tagging, which may leave a non-text view as first responder so that every key is "for a control"; or the key monitor no longer being installed for the new window chrome. Reproduce it in a test at the level of the routing function with the first responder the real window has after launch and after a click in each region, then fix. Add a launch-level check in the debug hook that sends real key events after: launch, a click in the slide grid, a click in the sidebar, a click in the inspector, a click in the bin, and after closing a sheet.
2. **"the focus of keyboard depend on last click to the section of the mouse. moving left / right button will move the selection but hit enter will bring the current live (slides and media in media bin)".** Decided with the owner:
   - Show has a keyboard section: the **slide grid** or the **media bin**, whichever was clicked last (the slide grid at launch and whenever a presentation is opened). The focused section is drawn with the design's focus treatment, quietly, so the operator can see where the arrows will act. A click in the sidebar, playlist or inspector does not take the arrows away from the last of those two sections; text fields take keys while they are being edited, as now.
   - **Slide grid:** Left and Right move the selection (Preview) one slide; Up and Down move by a row. **Return takes the selected slide live.** Nothing on any output changes from an arrow key.
   - **Media bin:** Left and Right (and Up and Down in a multi-row bin) move the selection between items and show it in the preview player. **Return sends the selected item live** to the layer the bin's switch says (the same as a double click). Arrows never change the live clip.
   - **Space stays "Next"**: it advances the live slide in one press, whatever section has the arrows, exactly as the Next button does. Shift-Space is Previous if that is free; say what you chose. The Next and Previous buttons and their key hints in the transport bar show the keys that really do it.
   - Blackout, Clear and the other transport keys are unchanged and work from either section.
   - This changes what the arrow keys did before (they advanced Live). Update the keyboard table in the Cues mode, the README of keys if any, and `docs/hands-on-checklist.md`; record the change in the change log in plain words because operators have habits.
3. **"when moving left / right button on keyboard, the slide will be moving as well and go to next song if current song is ended".** Moving the selection past the last slide of a presentation with Right selects the first slide of the next playable playlist item (and Left before the first slide selects the last slide of the previous one), opening that item in the grid, in Preview only. Headers are skipped; a countdown, media, loop or scripture item is selected as an item (Return takes it live as today). At the end of the playlist it stops. If the open presentation was opened from the library, not the playlist, it stops at the last slide. Space ("Next") past the last live slide keeps its existing rule for crossing into the next item.
4. **"when I clear the background, if the media bin background have live selected on, if I press next / previous, it will carry the background selected in the media bin. when clear the background. the live in media bin should be cleared as well".** This reverses a MEDIA-010 choice (Clear background hid the override and kept it). Now: **Clear background drops the live background override** chosen from the bin, the bin tile loses its LIVE mark, a "Play folder" run to the background ends, and Next, Previous and Go Live do not bring it back. What comes back on the next slide is that slide's own background, or the presentation's or theme's, as if no bin choice had been made. Clear foreground does the same for a foreground chosen from the bin, and Clear all for both. The inspector's override row disappears with it. Blackout is still reversible and drops nothing.
5. **"the slide thumbnail should have background so operator will know what background will be live in that slide".** Every slide thumbnail (grid and list, Show and Edit, Preview monitor) draws the background that slide will have when it goes live with no bin override: its own background if it has one, else the presentation's or theme's. A video background is drawn as a still frame taken off the main thread, cached by file and modification date, with a small film mark; a missing file draws the existing missing-media state. Thumbnails must not start players or hold decoders open; measure the grid with 200 slides and say what it costs. The Live monitor keeps showing what is really live, including a bin override.
6. **"right click to the thumbnail slide have option to clear background for that slide".** The slide thumbnail's context menu has "Clear this slide's background" whenever that slide has its own background, as one undo step, in Show and Edit. If "Use the presentation's background" already does this, keep one entry with the owner's wording. Also offer "Set background…" there (choose from the bin) since dropping is not always convenient. Editing the slide that is live does not change Live until it is taken live again, as with every edit; say so in the tooltip or the checklist.
7. **"let the warning 'Broadcast is not sending. The operator controls and the other screens are not affected.' fade out after 5 seconds".** The message card fades out 5 seconds after it appears. The fault must not become invisible: the screen's pill in the toolbar and its monitor keep their fault state for as long as the output is not sending, and clicking the pill shows the card again with Retry and Details. A new or different fault shows a new card. A card with a pointer over it or with keyboard focus inside does not fade until the pointer leaves. Timing comes from an injected clock.
