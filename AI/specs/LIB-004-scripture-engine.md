# Brief: LIB-004 — scripture engine: references, verse slides, translation files (rules only)

Read first: `CLAUDE.md` (licensing rule: do not silently bundle copyrighted material), `AGENTS.md`, `docs/MASTER-SPEC.md` rows PRES-09 and PRES-10 and open decision OPEN-05, `Sources/Domain/SlideContent.swift`, `Reflow.swift`, `SearchFolding.swift`.

Catalogue: PRES-09 (Bible integration: search by reference or keywords, verses into slides), PRES-10 (two translations side by side). Phase 4. The church this is built for uses English and Vietnamese.

## Scope

A new pure-Swift target `Sources/ScriptureEngine` (Foundation and `Domain` only). Delete both placeholders. No app, no persistence, no network, **and no Bible text in the repository** other than tiny hand-typed fixtures of a few verses for tests (use verses from the King James Version, which is public domain, and keep fixtures under 30 verses). Test first for every rule.

## What to build

1. **Canon**: the 66 books in Protestant order with chapter counts and verse counts per chapter for the standard English versification (derive the counts carefully and add a checksum test: total chapters 1,189; total verses 31,102 for the KJV versification). Stable book ids (`GEN`, `EXO`, … USFM-style three-letter codes).
2. **Book names**: English names, common abbreviations ("Gen", "Gn", "1 Cor", "1Co", "I Corinthians", "Song of Songs", "Canticles", "Ps", "Psalm", "Psalms", "Rev", "Revelation") and Vietnamese names and abbreviations as used in Vietnamese Protestant Bibles ("Sáng Thế Ký", "Sáng", "Xuất Ê-díp-tô Ký", "Thi Thiên", "Thi", "Ma-thi-ơ", "Mat", "Giăng", "1 Giăng", "Công Vụ", "Rô-ma", "Khải Huyền", and the rest of the 66). Matching ignores case, diacritics (use `SearchFolding`), hyphens, full stops and spacing, and accepts unique prefixes ("Phil" is ambiguous between Philippians and Philemon: report the ambiguity with both candidates rather than guessing; "Jud" likewise). Where you are not certain of a Vietnamese name or abbreviation, include the full name you are sure of, leave the abbreviation out, and list the uncertain ones in the report for a Vietnamese speaker to confirm.
3. **Reference parser**: `John 3:16`, `John 3:16-18`, `John 3:16–18` (en dash), `John 3:16,18,20-22`, `John 3`, `John 3-4`, `John 3:16-4:2`, `Jn 3.16`, `1 John 1:9`, `Ps 23`, `Psalm 119:105`, single-chapter books (`Jude 5` means verse 5; `Obadiah 3`), several references separated by semicolons (`John 3:16; Rom 8:28`), the Vietnamese forms (`Giăng 3:16`, `Thi Thiên 23:1-3`), and trailing or leading whitespace and stray punctuation. Output: a normalised list of verse ranges. Validation against the canon with operator-readable errors that say what is wrong ("John has 21 chapters", "John 3 has 36 verses"), never a crash, and a best-effort correction is **not** applied silently. A formatter turns a range back into display text in English or Vietnamese ("John 3:16–18", "Giăng 3:16–18").
4. **Translation file format**: a simple, documented, versioned JSON format for one translation: metadata (id, name, language, abbreviation, copyright statement, licence, source URL, whether it is public domain, versification note) and verses keyed by book, chapter, verse. A loader with validation (unknown books, duplicate verses, missing chapters are reported with counts; a partial translation such as a New Testament only is allowed and reported as such), and an importer from **USFM** text for one book (the common markers `\id`, `\c`, `\v`, `\p`, `\q`, `\s`, footnotes `\f … \f*` and cross-references `\x … \x*` stripped, word-level markers such as `\w … \w*` unwrapped; unknown markers are dropped and counted, never shown as text). Test the importer with a small hand-written USFM fixture.
5. **Verse lookup and slides**: given loaded translations and parsed ranges, return verses; turn them into `SlideContent` values under rules: one verse per slide by default, or up to N verses per slide, or split a long verse across slides at sentence or clause boundaries when it exceeds a character limit (never mid-word; Vietnamese has no special segmentation needs beyond whitespace); the reference shown on each slide ("John 3:16" or, for a slide covering part of a verse, "John 3:16a"); an optional second translation paired verse by verse into `SlideContent.secondary` (if the second translation lacks a verse, that slide's secondary is nil and it is reported); a title for the presentation. Verse numbers in the text are optional (superscript-style digits or plain "16 ").
6. **Keyword search** within loaded translations: folded text match of all words, with the reference and a snippet, ordered by canon position; a phrase in quotes matches consecutively; limited result count. In-memory is fine for this slice (a full Bible is about 31,000 verses); say how long a search takes on a synthetic full-size translation generated in the test.
7. **Research, written up in the report, with nothing downloaded into the repository**: which complete English and Vietnamese translations are public domain or freely licensed for redistribution inside an app, where authoritative machine-readable files can be obtained (prefer USFM or another documented format), and their exact licence terms. Candidates to check: King James Version; World English Bible; for Vietnamese, the 1925 or 1926 translation commonly called "Kinh Thánh Truyền Thống" or the Cadman version, and any others. State what you verified from a primary source (with URL) and what you could not confirm. Do not assume a text is public domain because it is old or widely copied online: say what evidence there is, and note that copyright term and status may differ by country.
8. **Docs**: `docs/features/LIB-004-scripture-engine.md` describing the rules, the file format, and the licensing position (no text bundled; the owner decides which translations to add, per OPEN-05).
